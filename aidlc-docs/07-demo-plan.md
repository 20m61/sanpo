# Demo Plan

## Demo Story

Two friends are going out together.

At first, the conversation feels a little heavy.  
sanpo joins as a cute AI third friend.

It appears as a small XR-like ghost on the screen and gently suggests topics.

## Demo Scenes

### Scene 1: Start Outing

Users create a shared outing session.

### Scene 2: Third Friend Appears

sanpo appears with a short greeting.

Example:

"今日はふたりのさんぽに、ちょっとだけ混ざるね。"

### Scene 3: Conversation Cue

sanpo suggests a topic.

Example:

"このあたり、前に話してた展示の雰囲気に近いかも。"

### Scene 4: Awkward Silence Help

User taps "help".

sanpo suggests a light topic.

Example:

"最近ちょっとハマってるもの聞いてみる？"

### Scene 5: Follow-up

After the outing, sanpo suggests a message.

Example:

"今日はありがと〜。ゆるく話せて楽しかった。またさんぽしよ。"

## Demo Goal

Show that sanpo reduces the pressure of two-person conversation by acting as a gentle third friend.
