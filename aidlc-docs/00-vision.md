# Vision

## Product Name

sanpo

## Tagline

2人でも、3人さんぽ。

## Concept

sanpo is a cute ghost-like AI third friend for two-person outings.

It runs on both users' smartphones during an outing and gently intervenes through notifications and XR presence based on time, location, outing plan, relationship context, and prior memories.

## Problem

Two-person outings can become socially heavy because each person feels responsible for maintaining conversation.

Common pain points:

- awkward silence
- difficulty finding topics
- pressure to read the mood
- difficulty changing topics
- emotional fatigue from one-on-one conversation

## Solution

sanpo externalizes part of the social labor by acting as a third friend.

It suggests conversation cues, light comments, timing hints, and contextual prompts.

## Human-Degrading Impact

sanpo makes users less dependent on their own conversation skills.

Users no longer need to fully handle:

- topic generation
- silence recovery
- social timing
- atmosphere creation
- conversation flow management
