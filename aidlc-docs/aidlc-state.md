## Current Intent

Two-person outings can feel socially heavy because conversation responsibility is concentrated between only two people.

sanpo introduces a cute AI third friend that walks with the two users through notifications and XR presence. It reduces awkward silence, creates conversation cues, and helps the two people feel as if they are enjoying the outing with a third friend.
